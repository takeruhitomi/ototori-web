Piano samples (*.pcm in this folder)

Salamander Grand Piano, by Alexander Holm.
Source: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Changes: Selected samples were converted from MP3 to 22,050 Hz mono 16-bit
little-endian PCM and limited to eight seconds. Pitches between the selected
recordings are generated by resampling during playback (js/audio-render.js).

These are the same files as android/app/src/main/assets/piano; keep the two
copies identical so the browser and Android builds sound the same.
